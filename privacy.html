Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 04, 2026

Welcome to C2SGame Studio. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you play our mobile and web games (the "Services").

Our games are free-to-play and are funded primarily through advertising. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to play our games.

1. Information Collected Automatically

While we do not collect direct personal identity information, our third-party advertising partners may automatically collect certain device-related information to serve relevant advertisements. This information may include:

  • Device properties (e.g., device model, operating system version, language settings).
  • IP address and coarse geolocation data (country/city level).
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Game performance and log data (e.g., crash logs, ad interactions).

2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

We integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs) to display ads in our games. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, or unique device identifiers to personalize ads and analyze traffic. Our main advertising network partner includes:

  • AppLovin (MAX): Used for ad mediation and serving. You can review their privacy policy here: AppLovin Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

4. Data Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area or California), you may have specific rights regarding your device data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You can manage your personalized ad preferences through your mobile device's system settings ("Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
